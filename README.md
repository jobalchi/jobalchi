## About me

I'm a mechanical engineering researcher at **UNIST**, working at the intersection of autonomous systems, control, and learning. My research focuses on how autonomous vehicles can make robust decisions when other agents and the environment are uncertain. Before joining UNIST, I studied Robotics Convergence Engineering at **Hanyang University ERICA**.
